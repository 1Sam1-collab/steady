Privacy Policy

Steady keeps everything you enter on your own device. We don't collect, upload or sell anything.

What Steady stores

The products you pick, their start dates, your reminder time, your weekly ratings, and any photos you choose to add. Ratings and settings are kept in your browser's local storage. Photos are shrunk and kept in your browser's IndexedDB storage.

Where it goes

Nowhere. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Steady loads its own files and two fonts from Google Fonts, and nothing you enter is sent with those requests.

Calendar reminders

If you tap "Add to calendar", Steady creates a calendar file on your device. It contains product names and your reminder time, and goes only where you choose to open it.

Doctor consultations

The "Consult a doctor" button opens the Be Bodywise app or website. Steady does not share your check-ins with Be Bodywise or anyone else.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings and tap "Delete all my data". Clearing your browser's site data also removes everything. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, deleted data can't be recovered.

Shared devices

Anyone who uses this browser on this device can open Steady and see what you've entered, including photos. Photos are optional.

Steady is a concept prototype made for the Mosaic Wellness Fellowship. It is not an official Be Bodywise product.
